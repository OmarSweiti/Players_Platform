# ADR-0007 — Time has two kinds: calendar dates and instants

**Status:** Accepted · 28 September 2026 · **Requirements:** SR-CORE-006, UX-007 · **Owner:** `0.4.8`

## Context
Every date is a naive `TIMESTAMP(3)`; SR-CORE-006 says "store timestamps in UTC", which is right for
instants and wrong for a birthday.

## Decision
Civil values — birth dates, passport expiry, contract and season boundaries — are `DATE` and `YYYY-MM-DD`.
Instants are `timestamptz(3)` and ISO-8601 UTC. Schedules store their IANA zone. Tenants carry a typed
IANA `timezone`; display uses the tenant's zone with the absolute time available where audit precision
matters. Rules take an injected clock. Legacy timestamps are converted only where their UTC provenance is
known.

## Alternatives rejected
UTC midnight for dates (a birthday shifts a day for anyone west of Greenwich); local time without a zone.

## Consequences
One migration; a birth date reads the same from UTC−10 and UTC+14; schedules survive daylight-saving changes.

## Revisit when
A domain needs a non-Gregorian calendar — a display decision, never a storage one.
